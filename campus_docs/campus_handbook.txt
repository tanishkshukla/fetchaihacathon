1. CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE, BUILDINGS & CLASSROOMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The campus is divided into four primary academic and research complexes:

1.1 Alan Turing Computer Science Block (Code: CS-BLOCK)
- Location: West Campus Promenade
- Total Floors: 4 Floors (Ground + 3)
- Ground Floor:
  * CS-101: 1st Year CSE Classroom (Capacity: 75)
  * CS-102: 1st Year CSE-Artificial Intelligence Section (Capacity: 75)
  * Programming Laboratory 1: 60 High-performance Ubuntu Workstations
  * Campus Central Server & Data Center Room
- 1st Floor:
  * CS-201: 2nd Year CSE Section A (Equipped with Smart Interactive Board)
  * CS-202: 2nd Year CSE Section B (Equipped with Dual Projectors)
  * Database & Cloud Systems Lab: High-speed Oracle & AWS Cloud Sandbox
  * Head of Department (HOD) Office & Departmental Meeting Chamber
- 2nd Floor:
  * CS-301: 3rd Year CSE Section A Lecture Hall
  * CS-302: 3rd Year CSE Section B Lecture Hall
  * AI & Machine Learning Research Center: Equipped with NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs
  * Faculty Cabins: Room CS-301 to CS-315
- 3rd Floor:
  * CS-401: 4th Year CSE Capstone & Project Hall
  * CS-402: 4th Year Research Seminar Room
  * Internet of Things (IoT) & Embedded Robotics Laboratory
  * Departmental Mini-Auditorium (Capacity: 180 seats)

1.2 Ramanujan Academic Block (Code: AB-MAIN)
- Location: Central Quadrangle
- Total Floors: 3 Floors (Ground + 2)

  * LT-2 (Lecture Theater 2): Multi-purpose Academic Lecture Hall
  * Student Academic Affairs & Administration Office
  * Accounts & Fee Clearance Desk
- 1st Floor:
  * Room 201: Foundation Coursework Lecture Hall
  * Room 202: Applied Engineering & CAD Drawing Classroom
  * Engineering Mechanics & Physics Laboratory
- 2nd Floor:
  * Room 301: Applied Mathematics Seminar Hall
  * Room 302: Humanities & Professional Communication Center
  * Office of the Dean of Academics

1.3 Vikram Sarabhai Electronics & Tech Block (Code: ECE-BLOCK)
- Location: East Wing near Tech Garden
- Total Floors: 3 Floors (Ground + 2)
- Ground Floor:
  * EC-101: 1st Year ECE Lecture Hall
  * Analog Electronics & Circuit Design Laboratory
  * Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Laboratory
- 1st Floor:
  * EC-201: 2nd Year ECE Lecture Hall
  * EC-202: Electronics Instrumentation Classroom
  * VLSI Semiconductor Design Center (Cadence & Synopsys Toolsets)
- 2nd Floor:
  * EC-301: 3rd Year ECE Lecture Hall
  * EC-401: Final Year ECE Advanced Wireless Communications Hall

1.4 Central Knowledge & Library Complex (Code: CENTRAL-LIB)
- Location: North Campus
- Total Floors: 2 Floors
- Ground Floor:

4. ACADEMIC POLICIES, ATTENDANCE & EXAMINATION GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Attendance Minimum Requirement:
- Mandatory minimum attendance: 75% in every individual theory and practical subject.
- Condonation: Up to 10% condonation (minimum 65%) permitted solely on medical grounds with submission of a Government Medical Officer certificate within 5 days of absence.
- Students with attendance below 65% are strictly detained from appearing in End-Semester Examinations.

4.2 Evaluation Scheme:
- Internal Assessment: 40% weightage (Continuous Assessment Tests, Mid-Term Exam, Quizzes, Lab Assignments).
- End-Semester University Examination: 60% weightage.
- Passing Criteria: Minimum 40% in End-Semester Examination AND overall aggregate of 45% in internal + external evaluation.

4.3 Campus Wi-Fi & Digital Resources:
- SSID: `CITS-Student-5G` (Authentication via Student Roll Number and Portal Password).
- VPN Access: Available for accessing IEEE Xplore and Springer journals from off-campus hostels via `vpn.campus.edu.in`.
